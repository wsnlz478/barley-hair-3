Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

How does FUE hair transplant work?
FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) involves extracting individual hair follicles from the donor area (typically the back and sides of the head) and transplanting them to the recipient area. Our advanced microneedle technique uses 0.6-1.0mm incisions for minimal trauma and faster healing.
Will there be scarring with FUE?
With FUE, scarring is minimal. The tiny punch incisions heal completely and are typically barely noticeable, especially when your hair grows back. This is a major advantage of FUE over FUT, which leaves a linear scar.
How long is recovery with FUE?
With our microneedle FUE technique, most patients can return to desk work within 2-3 days. Complete healing takes about 1-2 weeks. We'll provide you with detailed aftercare instructions to ensure smooth healing.
Can I wear short hair after FUE?
Yes! One of the biggest advantages of FUE is that it doesn't leave a linear scar, so you can wear your hair very short without anyone noticing. This is particularly popular with patients who like short hairstyles.
What is the difference between FUE and FUT?
FUE extracts individual follicles, while FUT removes a strip of tissue. FUE has less scarring and faster recovery but may be more time-consuming for very large cases. During consultation, we'll recommend the best approach for your specific needs.
When will I see results from FUE?
You'll start seeing new hair growth at 3-4 months. Significant results at 6-9 months, and full, mature results at 12-18 months as the transplanted hair continues to grow and become thicker.
What is microneedle FUE?
Our microneedle technique uses ultra-fine 0.6-1.0mm needles for creating recipient sites. This minimizes trauma, reduces bleeding, and allows for more precise graft placement with faster healing times.
Can FUE be used for facial hair?
Absolutely! FUE is perfect for beard, mustache, and eyebrow transplants. Because it extracts individual follicles, we can place them precisely where needed for natural-looking facial hair.
How many FUE sessions will I need?
It depends on your goals and the size of the area. Many patients achieve great results with just one session. Larger restorations might be split into 2-3 sessions spaced several months apart.
Is FUE safe?
Yes! FUE is a very safe procedure when performed by experienced surgeons. The procedure is minimally invasive and done under local anesthesia. Serious complications are extremely rare.
