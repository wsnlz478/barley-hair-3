Our Story & Mission

Barley Microneedle Hair Transplant is China's leading medical group specializing in premium hair restoration services. Founded in 2006, we pioneered microneedle hair transplant technology in China and have been setting industry standards ever since.

Our mission is simple: to help people from around the world regain their confidence through natural-looking, permanent hair restoration using our patented microneedle technology.

With 30+ directly operated clinics in major Chinese cities, we provide comprehensive one-stop solutions including hair loss diagnosis, hair transplant surgery, medical hair care, and anti-hair loss treatments—all backed by our philosophy: "Good Doctors + Good Technology = Great Results."

Questions About Us

Learn more about our clinic and services

When was Barley Hair Transplant founded and what is your experience?
Founded in 2006, we are the pioneer and leader of microneedle hair transplant technology in China. With over 18 years of experience, we've successfully treated more than 100,000 patients from around the world and established 30+ direct-operated clinics across major Chinese cities.
What certifications and standards do you follow at Barley?
We maintain Sullivan-certified excellence and follow international standards in all our procedures. With 10+ patented technologies, we combine Chinese precision with Western protocols to deliver world-class results.
Where are Barley's clinics located in China?
We have 30+ direct-operated clinics across major Chinese cities including Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Chengdu, Hangzhou, Nanjing, and many more. All clinics maintain the same high standards and use our patented technologies.
What patented technologies does Barley Hair Transplant offer?
We hold 10+ patented technologies, including our revolutionary implant pen that allows 360° rotation for natural hair direction. Our microneedle technology uses 0.6-1.0mm incisions for minimal trauma, faster recovery, and higher density results.
How many successful procedures has Barley completed?
We've successfully completed over 100,000 procedures since 2006. Our patient base includes people from over 50 countries who trust us for our expertise, natural results, and excellent aftercare support.
What sets Barley apart from other hair transplant clinics?
Our unique advantages include 18+ years of pioneering experience, 30+ direct-operated clinics, 10+ patented technologies, Sullivan-certified excellence, English-speaking staff, and our revolutionary microneedle implant pen with 360° rotation capability.
Can I find Barley on Google or Bing?
Yes, but the best place to learn about all we offer is right here on our official website and during your free consultation! We believe speaking with you directly is the best way to understand how we can help you.
Do you have experience with international patients?
Yes! We've treated patients from over 50+ countries. Our English-speaking staff and dedicated international patient coordinator ensure your experience is smooth and comfortable from consultation through aftercare.
What is your approach to patient care and safety?
Patient safety and satisfaction are our top priorities. We maintain the highest international standards in all our procedures. Our clinics use advanced sterilization protocols, experienced medical team, and comprehensive aftercare ensure you are in excellent hands throughout your journey.
Can I get a free consultation?
Yes! All consultations are completely free with no obligation. During your consultation, we'll evaluate your hair loss, discuss your goals, and create a personalized treatment plan just for you.
