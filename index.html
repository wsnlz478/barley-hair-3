Barley Microneedle Implant Pen

Microneedle hair transplant, also known as implant pen technology, works by placing individual hair follicles into the hollow tip of the implant pen, then directly pushing the follicle into the pre-designed recipient area of the scalp. The pen can be rotated 360° to match natural hair growth direction, significantly reducing trauma and patient pain while avoiding secondary follicle damage.

Smaller incision (0.6-1.0mm) - 1/3 smaller
Minimal trauma, fast recovery
Wash hair in 24 hours
20-30% higher density
360° rotation for natural direction

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

What makes Barley Hair Transplant different from other clinics in China?
Founded in 2006, we are the pioneer and leader of microneedle hair transplant technology in China. With 30+ direct-operated clinics, 10+ patented technologies, and Sullivan-certified excellence, we've successfully treated over 100,000 patients from around the globe. Our revolutionary implant pen ensures 360° rotation for natural hair direction and minimal trauma.
Do you accept international patients and offer English consultation?
Absolutely! We have English-speaking staff available for consultations and throughout your entire treatment journey. Many international patients travel to us from the USA, Canada, UK, Australia, South Korea, and beyond for our world-class expertise and competitive pricing.
How long does a hair transplant procedure typically take at Barley?
The duration depends on the number of grafts needed. Typically, a procedure takes 4-8 hours. During your free consultation, our doctors will evaluate your specific case and provide you with a precise timeline. Most patients can relax, watch movies, or listen to music throughout the comfortable procedure.
What payment options do you offer at Barley Hair Transplant?
We offer competitive pricing and flexible payment options to make your hair restoration accessible. Contact us for a personalized quote based on your specific needs. We accept multiple payment methods and can discuss payment plans during your consultation.
How do I book a free consultation with Barley?
Booking is easy! Simply visit our contact page, fill out the form, or reach out via WhatsApp, WeChat, or phone. Our international patient coordinator will help you schedule your consultation at one of our 30+ clinics across major Chinese cities.
Are the results from Barley Hair Transplant permanent?
Yes! The transplanted hair is taken from the donor area (back of the head) which is genetically resistant to DHT and hair loss. These hairs will continue to grow naturally for a lifetime. Our advanced microneedle technology ensures excellent graft survival and natural-looking results.
Can I find Barley on Google or Bing?
Yes, but the best place to learn about us is right here on our official website and during your free consultation! We'd love to speak with you directly to understand your goals and create a personalized treatment plan just for you.
Is there any downtime after a hair transplant at Barley?
With our microneedle technology, recovery is very fast. Most patients can return to desk work within 2-3 days. We'll give you detailed aftercare instructions to ensure your healing process is smooth and comfortable.
What services does Barley offer besides hair transplants?
We offer comprehensive hair restoration services including eyebrow transplants, beard transplants, hair thickening, and medical treatments like minoxidil and finasteride. We also have professional hair care programs to maintain your results and overall hair health.
Do you follow up with patients after the procedure?
Yes! We believe in comprehensive aftercare and ongoing support. We'll schedule follow-up appointments to monitor your healing and growth. For international patients, we also offer virtual follow-ups. We're with you every step of the way!
