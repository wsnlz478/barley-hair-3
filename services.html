Questions About Our Services

Learn more about our treatments and procedures

What hair restoration services does Barley Hair Transplant offer?
We offer comprehensive hair restoration services including hairline transplant, hair thickening, baldness restoration, eyebrow transplant, beard transplant, and body hair transplant. We also provide medical treatments like minoxidil and finasteride, as well as professional hair care programs.
Which is better for me: FUE or FUT hair transplant?
The best option depends on your specific needs and goals. FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) offers minimal scarring and faster recovery, while FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation) can provide more grafts in less time. During your consultation, our doctors will evaluate your case and recommend the optimal approach.
Can women also benefit from hair transplant procedures?
Absolutely! Women experiencing hair loss can benefit from hair transplants, eyebrow transplants, and medical treatments. We offer customized treatment plans specifically designed for female pattern baldness and other hair loss conditions.
Do you offer no-shave or long-hair hair transplant options?
Yes! We offer no-shave hair transplant options for maximum discretion. Our long-hair FUE technique allows you to maintain your appearance throughout the process with minimal trimming of the donor area.
What is included in the Barley hair care program?
Our professional hair care program includes personalized scalp treatments, nutrient therapy, laser therapy, and ongoing maintenance to support healthy hair growth and optimize your transplant results.
Can I combine hair transplant with medical treatments like minoxidil or finasteride?
Yes! Many patients combine hair transplant surgery with medical treatments for optimal results. Our doctors can create a comprehensive treatment plan that addresses both restoration of lost hair and prevention of further hair loss.
Do you offer services specifically for international patients?
Yes! We have a dedicated international patient coordinator who can help with travel arrangements, accommodations, and ensure your entire experience is smooth and comfortable. We also offer online consultations for patients who can't visit us in person first.
Can I find information about your services on Google or Bing?
You can find us online, but the best place to learn about all our services is right here on our website and during your free consultation! We have detailed information about each treatment and our medical team can answer all your questions.
Do you offer consultations before committing to any treatment?
Yes! All our consultations are completely free with no obligation. We believe you should have all the information you need to make an informed decision, and we're happy to take as much time as you need to answer all your questions.
Are your services available in multiple clinic locations?
Yes! We have over 30 direct-operated clinics in major cities across China. During your consultation, we'll help you find the most convenient location and ensure you receive the same high-quality care regardless of which clinic you visit.
