Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

What does a professional hair care program include?
Our professional hair care programs typically include personalized scalp treatments, nutrient therapy, specialized shampoos and conditioners, laser therapy (when appropriate), and ongoing maintenance plans tailored to your specific hair type and needs.
Can hair care programs help prevent hair loss?
Yes! A good professional hair care program can help maintain scalp health, strengthen existing hair, and in some cases help slow down or prevent further hair loss. When combined with medical treatments like minoxidil or finasteride, they can be even more effective.
How often should I get professional hair care treatments?
It depends on your specific program and needs. Some treatments may be monthly, while others follow a different schedule. During your consultation, we'll recommend a personalized treatment schedule based on your hair goals.
Can I do professional hair care at home?
Yes! We can provide you with professional-grade products and guidance on how to care for your hair at home. Some treatments are best done in-clinic, but we'll create a comprehensive plan that includes both professional treatments and home care.
Do hair care programs work with hair transplant surgery?
Yes! In fact, they work very well together. Professional hair care can help maintain your existing hair and optimize the health of your scalp and hair, creating an ideal environment for transplanted hair to thrive.
Are professional hair care products better than store-bought?
Professional-grade products typically have higher quality ingredients, more effective formulations, and are specifically designed for hair and scalp health. However, the best product depends on your specific hair type and needs. We'll recommend what's best for you.
How can I improve the overall health of my hair?
Good hair health starts from the inside! Eat a balanced diet with enough protein, iron, and vitamins. Also, avoid excessive heat and chemical treatments, keep your scalp clean, and protect your hair from sun damage. During consultation, we can give you personalized tips.
What's the best way to wash my hair?
It depends on your hair type! Generally, use lukewarm (not hot) water, gently massage shampoo into your scalp, and condition primarily the ends. How often you wash depends on your scalp—some people need daily, others every few days. We'll give you personalized advice.
Can diet really affect my hair health?
Absolutely! Your hair needs proper nutrition to grow and be healthy. Protein, iron, biotin, zinc, and vitamins D and B are all important for hair health. During consultation, we can discuss dietary factors and supplements that might help.
What is laser therapy for hair?
Low-level laser therapy (LLLT) uses lasers to stimulate the hair follicles and potentially improve hair density. It's sometimes used alongside other treatments. During consultation, we can discuss if this would be beneficial for your specific situation.
