Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

What are the treatment options for hair loss?
We offer a comprehensive range of hair loss treatment options including medical treatments like minoxidil and finasteride, hair transplant surgery (both FUE and FUT), professional hair care programs, and in some cases, laser therapy. During your consultation, we'll create a personalized plan for you.
How do I know which treatment is right for me?
During your free consultation, we'll assess your hair loss pattern, donor supply, overall health, and personal goals. Based on this comprehensive evaluation, we'll recommend the most appropriate treatment or combination of treatments for your specific situation.
Can I combine medical treatment with transplant surgery?
Yes! In fact, this is often recommended for optimal results. Medical treatments like minoxidil or finasteride can help preserve your existing hair while the transplant restores what's already been lost. We can create a comprehensive plan combining both approaches.
When should I start hair loss treatment?
The earlier the better! It's always easier to preserve existing hair than to restore what's been lost. If you've noticed hair thinning or loss, it's a good time to schedule a consultation to discuss your options and create a plan.
Is hair loss treatment effective for both men and women?
Yes! We have treatment options for both men and women. While the treatments and approaches may differ slightly, the goal is the same: to preserve existing hair and restore what's been lost. We customize treatment plans for each individual patient.
What results can I expect from hair loss treatment?
Results vary depending on the treatment and individual, but most patients see slowed or stopped hair loss with medical treatments, and permanent, natural hair restoration with transplant surgery. During consultation, we'll discuss realistic expectations for your specific situation.
Is there any way to prevent hair loss?
While you can't change your genetics, early treatment can slow or stop genetic hair loss. Also, maintaining a healthy diet, avoiding harsh chemical treatments, and managing stress can help keep your hair healthy. During consultation, we can discuss prevention strategies.
What if I've tried treatments before that didn't work?
Not to worry! Many treatments work better when combined, or you might need a different approach entirely. During consultation, we'll evaluate what you've tried before and create a new plan that's personalized for your needs.
Can hair loss be a sign of something more serious?
Most hair loss is genetic, but sudden or unusual hair loss can sometimes be related to medical conditions. During consultation, we'll ask about your health history and help determine if further evaluation is needed.
Are hair care products worth the investment?
Good quality hair care products can help maintain the health of your existing hair, but they won't restore lost hair. During consultation, we can recommend professional hair care products that complement your treatment plan.
