Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

How does eyebrow transplant work?
Eyebrow transplant involves carefully transplanting individual hair follicles from the scalp donor area to the eyebrow region. Using our microneedle technology, we place each follicle at precise angles and directions that match natural eyebrow growth patterns.
How many grafts are needed for eyebrow restoration?
Typically, 100-300 grafts per eyebrow are needed, depending on your desired thickness and the existing eyebrow hair you have. During your consultation, we'll design your ideal eyebrow shape and determine the exact number of grafts needed.
Can you design a natural looking eyebrow shape?
Yes! Our doctors specialize in creating natural looking eyebrow designs tailored to your face shape, features, and personal preferences. We consider your natural brow pattern, facial symmetry, and desired look to create beautiful, natural results.
Do transplanted eyebrow hairs need to be trimmed?
Yes! Since the hair comes from the scalp, it will continue to grow like scalp hair. You'll need to trim your transplanted eyebrows regularly to maintain the desired length and shape. This is a simple part of maintenance.
How long does it take to see eyebrow transplant results?
You'll start seeing new growth at 3-4 months. Significant results will be visible at 6-9 months, and full, mature results at 12-18 months as the transplanted hairs continue to grow and establish.
Is eyebrow transplant painful?
No! We use local anesthesia to numb the donor and recipient areas completely. Most patients report minimal discomfort during the procedure and find it relatively comfortable.
How long is recovery for eyebrow transplant?
Most patients can resume light activities within a couple of days. There might be some redness or minor scabbing for the first week or so. We'll give you detailed aftercare instructions to make healing as smooth as possible.
Can I wear makeup after eyebrow transplant?
We'll give you specific instructions, but generally you'll need to avoid makeup on the transplanted area for the first week or so to let it heal properly. Once fully healed, you can wear makeup as usual if you want.
Can I choose what shape of eyebrows to get?
Yes! During consultation, we'll discuss your goals, assess your facial structure, and design eyebrows that complement your features perfectly. We'll work with you to create the ideal shape and thickness you're looking for.
Can eyebrow transplant fix overplucked or thin eyebrows?
Yes! Eyebrow transplant is perfect for restoring eyebrows that have been overplucked, are naturally thin, or have been lost for other reasons. We can fill in sparse areas and restore full, beautiful eyebrows.
