Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

Can you design a natural looking hairline?
Yes! Our doctors specialize in creating natural looking hairlines tailored to your face shape, age, and personal preferences. Using our 360° implant pen, we ensure each hair grows at the correct angle for a completely natural appearance.
How many grafts are typically needed for hairline restoration?
It varies depending on your specific situation and goals. Typically, hairline restoration requires 800-2500 grafts. We'll assess your needs during your free consultation and give you a precise recommendation.
How long does it take to see final hairline results?
You'll start seeing new hair growth at 3-4 months. By 6 months, you'll see significant progress, and the final results will be visible at 12-18 months as the hair continues to mature and thicken.
Can women have hairline transplant surgery?
Yes! Women experiencing hair loss or who want to improve their hairline can benefit from hair transplant surgery. We have extensive experience treating female patients and creating beautiful, natural results.
Will my hairline look too "pluggy" or artificial?
No! Our microneedle technology and advanced techniques ensure completely natural looking results. We use single follicular units and place them at natural angles and densities, avoiding the "doll head" look from older techniques.
Can I style my transplanted hair normally?
Yes! Once the transplanted hair has fully grown, you can style, cut, dye, and treat it just like your natural hair. It will grow and behave exactly like your existing hair.
Is hairline transplant painful?
No! We use local anesthesia to numb the donor and recipient areas completely. Most patients report minimal discomfort during the procedure and find it relatively comfortable.
How long is recovery for hairline transplant?
Most patients can resume light activities and desk work within a few days. There might be some redness or minor scabbing for the first week or so. We'll give you detailed aftercare instructions to make healing as smooth as possible.
Can I choose how low or high my hairline is?
Yes! During consultation, we'll discuss your preferences and design a hairline that complements your face shape, age, and natural features. We'll work with you to create the perfect look you're going for.
Can I combine hairline transplant with medical treatments?
Yes! Many patients combine hairline transplant with medical treatments like minoxidil or finasteride to preserve their existing hair while restoring their hairline. We'll create a comprehensive plan tailored to your specific needs.
