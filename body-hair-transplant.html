Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

What areas can be treated with body hair transplant?
Body hair transplant can be used for various areas including the chest, abdomen, pubic area, and more. We can restore or enhance body hair in specific areas for a more masculine or aesthetic. During consultation, we'll discuss your goals and what's achievable.
Where do the grafts come from for body hair transplant?
The donor hair typically comes from the scalp donor area, which has the best quality and survival rate. In some cases, if more hair can be used, but scalp hair is always preferred when possible.
How many grafts are typically needed for body hair restoration?
It varies greatly depending on the area and desired density. For the chest, 500-2000 grafts may be needed. For smaller areas, fewer grafts are required. During your consultation, we'll assess your needs and create a personalized plan.
Will transplanted body hair look natural?
Yes! Using our microneedle technology, we place grafts at natural angles and directions that match the existing body hair growth patterns. This creates completely natural looking results that blend seamlessly with your existing hair.
How long does it take to see body hair transplant results?
You'll start seeing new growth at 3-4 months. Significant results will be visible at 6-9 months, and full, mature results at 12-18 months as the transplanted hairs continue to grow and establish.
Is body hair transplant painful?
No! We use local anesthesia to numb the donor and recipient areas completely. Most patients report minimal discomfort during the procedure and find it relatively comfortable.
How long is recovery for body hair transplant?
Most patients can resume light activities within a few days. The transplanted area might be a bit red or scabbed for the first week or so. We'll give you detailed aftercare instructions and a timeline for resuming all activities.
Do I need to shave the recipient area for body hair transplant?
We'll discuss this during consultation depending on the area and your preferences. In some cases, we might need to trim the area slightly for better access and precision, but in other cases, we might be able to work around existing hair.
Can transplanted body hair be trimmed or shaved?
Yes! Once the hair has fully grown and established, you can trim, shave, or style it just like your natural body hair. It will continue to grow with characteristics matching your donor hair.
Can I get multiple areas done at once?
Depending on the size of the areas and your donor supply, we can sometimes treat multiple areas in a single session. During consultation, we'll assess what's feasible and create a treatment plan that works for you.
